About GigRadar

Built for South Africa's
squads and crews

GigRadar started with a simple, familiar problem: at a festival, a hike, or a night out, it's easy to lose track of the people you came with. Signal drops, batteries die, and one person wandering off can turn a good night into a stressful one.

We built GigRadar to fix that — a safety layer for the moments that matter, whether you're navigating a crowded festival ground or a quiet trail with patchy signal.

What we do

GigRadar keeps your squad visible to each other. Everyone in your group can see where everyone else is, set a safe zone around your group or campsite, and reach the whole squad instantly if something goes wrong — all built around the way South Africans actually gather: eMbumnandini and out on the trail.

Where we're headed

This is the beginning of a bigger mission — we're working toward a version of GigRadar that stays connected to your squad even when the internet doesn't. Safety shouldn't depend on how many bars you have.

Who's behind it

GigRadar is built by LTC Sons (Pty) Ltd, from Umlazi, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, led by founder Mfundo Chiliza.
