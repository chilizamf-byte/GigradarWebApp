Data Deletion

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your GigRadar account and the personal data associated with it, in line with your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

How to request deletion

Send a deletion request to mfundo@gigradar.africa from the email address linked to your GigRadar account, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Include your registered name so we can locate your account.

We verify each request against the account on file before deleting anything, to stop someone else from deleting your data on your behalf.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details — name, email address, and authentication credentials
  • Your location history and any active safe zone data
  • Your emergency contact details
  • Your squad membership records
  • Your device push token

Squad chat messages already auto-delete after 24 hours regardless of account status, so there is normally nothing left to remove there by the time a request is processed.

Turnaround time

We process deletion requests within 30 days of verifying your identity. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done.

What we may retain

Where required to meet a legal, security, or fraud-prevention obligation, we may retain a limited record (for example, that a request was made and honoured) for as long as that obligation applies. This does not include your location history, squad data, or emergency contact details, which are deleted in full.

Deleting the app

Uninstalling GigRadar from your device stops it from collecting new data, but does not delete your account or the data already stored — you still need to submit a deletion request as above.
